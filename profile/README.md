# Flowful AI

**Your busywork, handled by AI.** We are a small AI agency in Brussels and Lyon. We build chatbots, an AI helpdesk and custom AI software for SMBs, with clear pricing and EU hosting.

[Website](https://flowful.ai) · [Packages](https://flowful.ai/packages/) · [Case studies](https://flowful.ai/projects/) · [Book a 30-min call](https://flowful.ai/contact/) · [contact@flowful.ai](mailto:contact@flowful.ai)

## What we ship

| Package | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Web Chatbot](https://flowful.ai/packages/web-chatbot/) | Answers visitor questions 24/7, captures leads, routes the complex ones to your team |
| [TicketFlow](https://flowful.ai/packages/ticketflow/) | AI-first helpdesk, an alternative to Zendesk and Freshdesk: ticket queue, automatic replies, summaries, sentiment |
| [Internal Chatbot](https://flowful.ai/packages/internal-chatbot/) | A company ChatGPT: your team finds answers and follows processes faster |

Beyond the packages: custom agents, integrations, internal tools and MCP servers. See the [case studies](https://flowful.ai/projects/) for what that looks like in practice.

## How we work

1. **Discovery call**, 30 minutes, to map the workflow and what success looks like.
2. **Prototype** on your real data, so you can judge the result before committing.
3. **Production**: monitoring, security, maintenance, hosted in the EU or on your infrastructure.

## Open source

Most client work is private. What we open is the tooling we built for ourselves and found broadly useful.

- [cad-skill](https://github.com/flowful-ai/cad-skill): a Claude Code skill that turns a description into a parametric, 3D-printable CadQuery model, with STL export and previews.
- [overhang](https://github.com/flowful-ai/overhang): enclosures and brackets designed by chat. Print-ready STL or 3MF in under a minute, self-hosted. [Try it](https://overhang.flowful.ai).
- [tasksflow](https://github.com/flowful-ai/tasksflow): task management with an MCP server, so your AI agent works the same board you do. GitHub and Slack integrations, Bun.
